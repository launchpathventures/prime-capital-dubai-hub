# AML Compliance Quiz: Sanctions, Screening & TFS

**Purpose:** Verify your understanding of sanctions screening procedures, TFS implementation, and the correct response to different screening results.

**Format:** 5 multiple-choice questions
**Passing Score:** 80% (4 out of 5 correct)
**Time Limit:** No time limit (typically 8 minutes)
**Retakes:** Unlimited

---

## Questions

### Question 1
**Cygnus Scan returns a partial name match during client onboarding — the name and nationality match a UN Consolidated List entry, but the date of birth differs. What is the correct immediate action?**

A) Proceed with the transaction since the DOB does not match — it is likely a false positive
B) Reject the client immediately and file a CNMR
C) Suspend the transaction, withhold services, and file a PNMR via goAML within 5 business days
D) Ask the client to confirm whether they are the listed person

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A partial match — where some identifiers match but you cannot confirm or rule out — requires immediate suspension of the transaction and filing a PNMR within 5 business days. You cannot assume it is a false positive based on one differing identifier. The EOCN will respond to the PNMR with guidance. Asking the client could constitute tipping off.

---

### Question 2
**What are the four steps of Prime Capital's TFS implementation process, in order?**

A) Screen → Freeze → Report → Monitor
B) Register (EOCN NAS) → Screen → Implement TFS Measures → Report
C) Identify → Verify → Escalate → Document
D) Detect → Suspend → Investigate → Close

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The TFS 4-step implementation is: (1) Register — Prime Capital is registered with EOCN Notification Alert System for automated list updates; (2) Screen — within 24 hours of updates, before onboarding, at KYC reviews, before transactions, daily; (3) Implement TFS Measures — freeze within 24 hours for confirmed match, suspend for partial; (4) Report — CNMR or PNMR via goAML within 5 business days.

---

### Question 3
**After thorough examination, a screening alert is determined to be a false positive — the client is clearly not the designated person. What documentation is required?**

A) File a PNMR with the FIU noting the false positive determination
B) No documentation needed — simply proceed with the transaction
C) Document the false positive determination internally — no external report required
D) File an STR explaining why the match was dismissed

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** False positives require internal documentation only — record the screening alert, the analysis performed, and the conclusion that it is not a match. No external report (CNMR, PNMR, or STR) is required. However, the documentation must be retained in case of audit or regulatory inquiry.

---

### Question 4
**A confirmed match is discovered for an existing client with AED 2 million in funds held with the firm. Within what timeframe must the funds be frozen?**

A) Immediately upon discovery — no specific timeframe
B) Within 24 hours — with no prior notice to the client
C) Within 5 business days, concurrent with the CNMR filing
D) After receiving confirmation from the EOCN

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Upon a confirmed match, all funds and assets must be frozen within 24 hours — with no prior notice to the client. This is followed by filing a CNMR via goAML within 5 business days. Notifying the client before freezing would constitute tipping off. You do not need to wait for EOCN confirmation to freeze — the confirmed match itself triggers the obligation.

---

### Question 5
**Which statement about the lifting of TFS freezing measures is correct?**

A) Freezing measures can be lifted after 90 days if no further evidence emerges
B) The Compliance Officer can lift freezing measures based on the client's explanation
C) Freezing measures can only be lifted when the person is delisted from sanctions lists or EOCN communicates cancellation
D) A court order is required to lift freezing measures

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Freezing measures remain indefinitely until one of two things happens: (1) the designated person is delisted from the applicable sanctions list, or (2) EOCN communicates cancellation of the freezing directive through goAML. No one at Prime Capital — including senior management — can unilaterally lift a freeze. The client's explanation or the passage of time does not change this.
