# Completing the Deal File in TopStreet AI (Agents)

## Introduction

When you win a deal, your admin team needs your deal details and a set of documents to take it to completion. Chasing these by message wastes your time and theirs. TopStreet AI gives each won deal one short list of exactly what is still needed, and a button to send the file to your admin team when your part is complete.

This lesson is about your part of the file. The Dubai process itself (SPA, Form F, NOC, DLD transfer) is in the earlier modules of this competency.

**Time:** about 20 minutes, with the app open beside you.

---

## 1. Find your deal

1. Open **Deals** in TopStreet AI.
2. Open the deal you won. You only see your own deals.
3. Look for **What this file needs** at the top of the deal. The deal has Details, People and Documents tabs; the Documents tab has the list under "Your part".

It says how many things you still need to add. Each row has a short name, a status word and one button. The highlighted button is the next step.

### The status words

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **To do** | You still need to add this |
| **Done** | It is in. Your admin team may still check it |
| **Later** | Not needed yet. It is needed when the deal closes |
| **Optional** | Add it if you have it. The file does not wait for it |

---

## 2. Add the deal details

Enter the deal type, the lead type, the commission and the lead source once. When you change anything, a bar appears at the bottom of the page. Press **Save changes**. Nothing is saved until you press it.

**Why this matters:** these details decide which documents are asked for, and your admin team uses them for their records. A wrong deal type means the wrong list.

---

## 3. Add who is on the file and their documents

1. Add each person's details on the **People** tab.
2. On the **Documents** tab, press **Add** on each row that asks for a document and choose the file.
3. The row turns to **Done** when the document is in.

For a buyer who lives in the UAE the list typically asks for the passport, the Emirates ID, the residence visa and the signed booking form. The list on your deal is the one to follow: it is built for that deal type.

**Check before you upload:**
- The whole page is visible, nothing is cut off
- The document is readable, not a dark photo
- The name matches the buyer on the deal
- It is the current document, not an expired one

## 4. Send it to your admin team

Under "Before you send" is what the file needs. Until it is all in, the page says what is still to add. When it is, press **Send to the admin team**. Items under "When the deal closes" are marked **Later**: add those when you have them.

---

## 5. If something is sent back

Your manager can ask for a new copy of a document and will write why, for example "the photo page is cut off".

- You are told in the app, and by email if you have email notifications on.
- Open the deal. The row shows the reason.
- Add the new copy on the same row.
- When the changes are in, press **Send it again**.

The new copy replaces the old one. The old one is kept in the history, so nothing is lost.

**Turn on notifications on your phone** (Settings, then allow notifications) so you hear about a send-back straight away.

## 6. An older deal that asks "What kind of deal is this?"

Deals won before the new list may show one question at the top: Off-plan, Secondary or Rental. One option may be marked **Suggested**, taken from the property on the deal.

1. Choose the one that fits the deal.
2. Press **Save changes**.
3. The deal gets its list by itself.

If you are not sure, ask your manager. Do not guess: the type decides which documents are asked for.

---

## Common mistakes

1. Adding details but not pressing **Save changes**
2. Choosing the deal type to make a list shorter, not because it is right
3. Uploading a partial or blurry scan, then waiting to be told
4. Forgetting to press **Send to the admin team** (or **Send it again** after a send-back)
5. Adding a new copy outside the app (by message) and not on the row
6. Leaving phone notifications off and missing a send-back

---

## Key takeaways

1. One list per deal: **What this file needs** is the single place to look
2. Press **Save changes** when the bar appears
3. When your part is complete, press **Send to the admin team**
4. A send-back comes with a reason: fix the row it names
5. A new copy replaces the old one, and the old one is kept in the history
6. For an older deal, choose the deal type and save
