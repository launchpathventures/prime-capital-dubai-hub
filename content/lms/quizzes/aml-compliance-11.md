## Questions

### Question 1
**You notice that a client's declared income does not match the transaction amount — they earn AED 15,000/month but are purchasing a AED 3 million property with cash. What is your first action?**

A) File an STR directly on the goAML platform
B) File an ISTR (Internal Suspicious Transaction Report) with the Compliance Officer within 24 hours
C) Ask the client to explain the discrepancy before taking any action
D) Wait to see if Cygnus Scan returns any hits before reporting

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Your first action is always to file an ISTR with the Compliance Officer — not to file an external report (that is the CO's responsibility) and not to investigate further on your own (the CO determines next steps). The ISTR must be filed within 24 hours of establishing suspicion. The income-to-purchase discrepancy, combined with cash payment, is a clear red flag that warrants immediate internal reporting.

### Question 2
**What is the key difference between an STR/SAR and a CNMR?**

A) STR is for large transactions; CNMR is for small transactions
B) STR/SAR is for suspicious activity without a sanctions match; CNMR is for a confirmed match to a designated person on a sanctions list
C) STR is filed internally; CNMR is filed externally
D) STR is filed by the agent; CNMR is filed by the Compliance Officer

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The key distinction: STR/SAR is filed when there are identified red flags or suspicion of ML/TF that do NOT involve a confirmed or partial match to the UAE Local Terrorist List or UN Consolidated List. CNMR is filed specifically when there is a confirmed match to a designated person on those lists. Both are filed externally via goAML by the Compliance Officer.

### Question 3
**A property transaction involves a cash payment of AED 60,000 as a booking deposit. No other suspicious indicators are present. Which report is required?**

A) STR — cash transactions are always suspicious
B) CNMR — cash transactions require sanctions reporting
C) REAR — cash payment ≥ AED 55,000 in a property transaction triggers a Real Estate Activity Report
D) No report — AED 60,000 is below the reporting threshold

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A REAR (Real Estate Activity Report) is required for the purchase or sale of freehold real estate involving single physical cash transactions of AED 55,000 or more (or linked transactions totalling this amount). AED 60,000 exceeds this threshold. A REAR is not an STR — it is a mandatory activity report regardless of whether there is suspicion. The CO files it via goAML.

### Question 4
**After the Compliance Officer files an STR about a client, what must happen immediately?**

A) Terminate the business relationship and return all funds
B) Classify the client as high-risk, implement enhanced monitoring, and delay transactions where possible without tipping off
C) Inform the client that there will be delays due to "regulatory processing"
D) Freeze all funds and assets for 90 days pending investigation

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** After an STR is filed: (1) classify the client as high-risk immediately, regardless of prior classification; (2) implement enhanced monitoring; (3) delay transaction execution where possible — without tipping off the client; and (4) follow any FIU instructions. Fund freezing (option D) is for sanctions matches (CNMR), not STRs. Informing the client about "regulatory processing" risks tipping off.

### Question 5
**What is the minimum monetary threshold for filing an Internal Suspicious Transaction Report (ISTR)?**

A) AED 55,000 — aligned with the REAR threshold
B) AED 100,000 — significant transactions only
C) AED 1,000,000 — only for high-value suspicious transactions
D) There is no minimum threshold — report on suspicion, regardless of amount

**Correct Answer:** D
**Explanation:** There is no minimum monetary threshold for filing an ISTR. If you suspect ML/TF, you must report it regardless of the transaction amount. A AED 5,000 transaction that triggers genuine suspicion requires the same reporting as a AED 5 million one. The obligation is triggered by suspicion, not by amount.
