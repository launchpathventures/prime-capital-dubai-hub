Welcome to Module 5.9. This module covers one of the most operationally critical aspects of AML compliance: sanctions screening. Every client, every transaction party, every beneficial owner must be screened against international sanctions lists before you proceed. And the results of that screening determine your next steps — which may include freezing assets within 24 hours.

Prime Capital uses Cygnus Scan for real-time screening against the UN Consolidated List, UAE Local Terrorist List, OFAC, EU, HM Treasury, and other sanctions databases. You need to understand four possible outcomes: confirmed match, partial match, false positive, and negative match. Each triggers a different response with specific deadlines.

The Targeted Financial Sanctions framework gives you a 4-step process: Register with the EOCN notification system, Screen continuously, Implement freezing measures when required, and Report via goAML within 5 business days.

The deadlines here are not flexible. A confirmed match means freeze within 24 hours and report within 5 business days. A partial match means suspend immediately and report within 5 business days. These are legal obligations.

This module turns screening from a background process into an operational skill you can execute with confidence.
