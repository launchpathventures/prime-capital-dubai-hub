## Questions

### Question 1
**You reject a document. What must you include?**

A) Nothing  
B) A short reason the agent can act on  
C) A copy of the client's passport  
D) A new deal number  

**Correct Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** The reason is shown to the agent on the deal so they know what to fix.

---

### Question 2
**What happens if you record a screening as Escalate or Reject?**

A) Nothing, it is only a note  
B) The file cannot be closed  
C) The agent is removed from the deal  
D) The file is deleted  

**Correct Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Escalating or rejecting stops the file from being closed. Your compliance procedures decide what happens next.

---

### Question 3
**What does Download everything give you?**

A) Only the missing documents  
B) Every document for the deal in one zip  
C) The billing records  
D) A copy of the agent's notes  

**Correct Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** It gives you every document for the deal in one zip.

---

### Question 4
**When is Approve available?**

A) Always  
B) Once the agent has sent the file  
C) Only for billing managers  
D) After the deal moves to Aftercare  

**Correct Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Until then the page says what is outstanding. If something is missing or not reviewed when you approve, it names it and asks you to confirm.

---

### Question 5
**Which is true of the billing steps?**

A) They create an invoice  
B) They stop a deal from closing  
C) They are for managers only and are recorded checkpoints  
D) Agents complete them  

**Correct Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Billing is managers-only. The steps are recorded with who and when, and do not create an invoice or block closing.
