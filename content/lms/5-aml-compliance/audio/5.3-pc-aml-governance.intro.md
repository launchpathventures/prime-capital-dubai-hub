Welcome to Module 5.3. You have learned what financial crime is and what the law requires. Now you need to understand how Prime Capital implements those requirements — and where you fit.

Prime Capital operates a three lines of defence model. The first line is you — the business, the front office, the consultants who interact with clients. You are responsible for KYC collection, applying due diligence, recognising red flags, and escalating concerns. The second line is the Compliance Officer, who reviews your escalations, files external reports, and maintains the overall programme. The third line is the independent audit function that tests whether the first two lines are working.

Here is the critical point: money laundering is most likely to be detected at the point of first contact. That is your desk. Your phone call. Your meeting. If you miss the indicators, the second and third lines may never see them.

This module also covers Prime Capital's prohibited business relationships — the client types that must never be onboarded, with no exceptions and no senior override. And you will learn about the no-retaliation policy that protects you when you report suspicious activity in good faith.

Your role in AML compliance is not administrative. It is operational. You are the front line.
