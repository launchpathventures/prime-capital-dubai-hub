Welcome to Module 5.10. This is where your pattern recognition skills are built. Prime Capital's AML policy contains over 60 red flag indicators — signals that a client or transaction may involve money laundering or terrorist financing. These are not theoretical. They are drawn from real cases, real investigations, and real convictions.

Red flags come in three categories: natural person indicators, legal entity indicators, and transaction indicators. A client who is reluctant to explain their source of funds is a red flag. A company registered at a shared mailbox address using a Gmail account is a red flag. A buyer who shows no interest in the property's quality and accepts above-market pricing without negotiation is a red flag.

No single flag is proof of crime. But multiple flags in the same transaction dramatically increase the probability that something is wrong.

This module also covers ten real estate ML/FT typology case studies from the policy — real patterns including smurfing, rapid buy and resell, shell companies, family members used as fronts, and exploitation of charities. Study these cases. They are the patterns you will encounter in practice.

Your ability to spot these patterns is what separates a compliant consultant from one who unknowingly facilitates crime.
