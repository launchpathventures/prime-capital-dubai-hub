## Questions

### Question 1
**You used Gemini to research average rental yields in JVC and got a response with specific percentages. A client asks you about JVC yields during a call. What should you do?**

A) Share the percentages from Gemini — it's a reliable source
B) Tell the client you'll get back to them with verified data from Property Finder or DXB Interact
C) Round the numbers up slightly to make JVC sound more attractive
D) Say "according to AI research" and share the figures

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AI-generated numbers should always be treated as rough estimates. For specific market data like rental yields, verify with authoritative sources (Property Finder, Bayut, DXB Interact) before sharing with clients. Your professional reputation depends on accuracy, not speed.

---

### Question 2
**You need to draft a follow-up email to a client named Ahmed who viewed properties in Dubai Marina last week. What's the correct way to use Gemini?**

A) "Write a follow-up email to Ahmed Al-Rashid at ahmed@email.com about the 2BR in Marina Gate he viewed on Tuesday"
B) "Write a follow-up email to a client who viewed 2BR apartments in Dubai Marina last week. Professional but warm tone, under 150 words."
C) Copy-paste Gemini's response directly into your email client and send
D) Ask Gemini to also pull Ahmed's contact details from your CRM

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Never input sensitive client information (names, email addresses, phone numbers) into AI tools. Use anonymized, generic prompts. Then personalize the draft yourself before sending — adding the client's name, specific property references, and personal touches.

---

### Question 3
**A colleague says "I don't need to learn the market anymore — I just ask Gemini everything." What's wrong with this approach?**

A) Nothing — AI is designed to replace the need for personal expertise
B) It's risky because over-reliance on AI means losing genuine market knowledge, and clients can sense when you lack real expertise
C) It's only a problem if the AI gives wrong answers
D) It's fine as long as they fact-check everything

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AI should supplement your expertise, not replace it. Clients pay for your professional judgment, market knowledge, and personal insight. Over-relying on AI creates dependency and erodes the genuine expertise that builds client trust. Keep learning, stay curious, and use AI to multiply your effectiveness — not substitute for it.

### Question 4
**Where can you access Google Gemini through your Prime Capital account?**

A) Only through the Gemini website at gemini.google.com
B) Through gemini.google.com, Gmail integration, Google Docs, and the Gemini mobile app
C) Only through a special Prime Capital AI portal
D) Through ChatGPT using your Google login

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Gemini is integrated across your Google Workspace — accessible via the web at gemini.google.com, within Gmail (compose window), Google Docs (side panel and inline), and the Gemini mobile app. Sign in with your Prime Capital Google account for all access points.

### Question 5
**You're preparing for a meeting with a first-time buyer from the UK. Which Gemini prompt would be most useful?**

A) "Tell me everything about Dubai real estate"
B) "I'm meeting a first-time buyer from the UK looking for a vacation home in Dubai. What questions should I prepare to ask? What concerns might they have?"
C) "Write my entire presentation for the meeting"
D) "Is Dubai a good place to invest?"

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Specific, contextual prompts produce the best results from AI. Prompt B provides context (first-time buyer, UK, vacation home) and asks for actionable outputs (questions to ask, concerns to prepare for). Vague prompts like "tell me everything" produce generic, unhelpful responses.

### Question 6
**After generating a WhatsApp message using Gemini for a client, what should you do before sending?**

A) Send it immediately — AI produces professional content
B) Read it, edit for tone, add personal touches referencing previous conversations, then send
C) Just check it for typos and send
D) Forward the AI output to your manager for approval first

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AI drafts are starting points, not final products. Always read the message, adjust the tone for your relationship with the client, add personal references the AI can't know (previous conversations, shared details), and inject your own voice. Generic AI messages make clients feel like they're getting template responses.

### Question 7
**A key limitation of AI tools like Gemini is that they:**

A) Can't understand English well
B) Can generate confident-sounding but incorrect information
C) Only work during business hours
D) Can't help with real estate topics

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AI generates responses based on patterns and can sometimes produce "hallucinations" — confident-sounding but factually incorrect information. This is why verification is critical. AI doesn't "know" things the way humans do; it predicts likely responses based on training data.

---

### Question 8
**Which of these tasks is AI LEAST reliable for?**

A) Drafting and improving written communication
B) Providing real-time, current property prices and availability
C) Translating content between languages
D) Summarizing large amounts of text

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AI struggles with real-time data like current property prices and availability, which change daily. For this information, use dedicated platforms like Property Finder, Bayut, or DXB Interact. AI excels at communication drafts, translations, and summarization — tasks that rely on language skills rather than live data.

### Question 9
**You want to use AI to help with a client's property search. Which input is appropriate?**

A) "Find 3BR apartments for my client Sarah Johnson, budget AED 2M, she works at DIFC and her Emirates ID is 784-XXXX"
B) "What are good 3BR apartment options in areas near DIFC for a budget of AED 2M? Compare Business Bay, Downtown, and DIFC itself."
C) "Here's my client's financial statement — can you assess if they can afford a 3BR?"
D) "My client's passport number is XX123456, can you check their visa status for property eligibility?"

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Never input sensitive client information into AI tools — no names, ID numbers, financial documents, or contact details. Use anonymized, general queries. Prompt B gets useful area comparisons without exposing any private client data. Handle sensitive information only through secure, approved company systems.

### Question 10
**When should you add AI to your morning routine?**

A) Replace your entire planning block with AI-generated task lists
B) Add a quick market scan during your 7:00-7:30 AM planning block — ask Gemini for recent Dubai real estate news to stay informed for client conversations
C) Don't use AI in the morning — save it for afternoons only
D) Use AI to automatically respond to all overnight messages

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Integrating a quick AI market scan into your existing morning planning block helps you stay informed for the day's client conversations. Ask Gemini for recent developments, then verify anything important. This supplements — not replaces — your morning routine. Never use AI to auto-respond to clients.
