Welcome to Module 5.12 — the final module in the AML Compliance competency. This module covers four topics that every consultant must understand: tipping off, cash policy, record keeping, and the penalty framework.

Tipping off is a federal crime. If you inform a client — directly or indirectly — that a suspicious transaction report has been filed, will be filed, or that an investigation is underway, you face minimum 6 months imprisonment and fines of AED 100,000 to AED 500,000. There is no fine-only option. This is the most commonly misunderstood aspect of AML compliance. Even a casual comment to a colleague who then tells the client can constitute tipping off.

Prime Capital discourages cash payments above AED 55,000 per transaction. Above that threshold, traceable payment methods — bank transfers and cheques — must be used. Cash at or above this level triggers REAR reporting to the FIU.

All compliance records must be retained for a minimum of 5 years. CDD documents, transaction records, ISTRs, training logs — everything.

And the penalty framework makes the consequences clear: individuals face up to 10 years imprisonment and fines up to AED 5 million. Companies face fines up to AED 50 million and, in the case of TF conviction, dissolution.

This is the framework that protects you — and the consequences when it is broken. Learn it. Follow it.
