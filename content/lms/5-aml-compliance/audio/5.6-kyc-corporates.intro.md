Welcome to Module 5.6. Corporate clients introduce a level of complexity that individual clients do not. Behind every company is a chain of ownership — shareholders who own other companies, which own other companies — and your job is to trace that chain all the way down to the natural person who ultimately benefits. That person is the Ultimate Beneficial Owner.

This module covers the corporate KYC form, which is significantly more detailed than the individual form. You will need to collect 12 documents — nearly double the individual requirement. You will learn how to trace ownership through layered structures using the 25 percent threshold: any natural person who owns 25 percent or more of the shares, directly or indirectly, is a UBO.

You will also learn the additional requirements for developer onboarding and external broker verification — both carry their own specific document requirements on top of the standard corporate KYC.

The key principle: companies do not launder money. People do. Your job is to see through the corporate structure to the person behind it.
