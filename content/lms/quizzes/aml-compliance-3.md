## Questions

### Question 1
**In Prime Capital's three lines of defence model, what is the primary responsibility of the first line (you)?**

A) Filing STR/SAR reports with the FIU via goAML
B) Conducting independent audits of AML controls
C) Client onboarding KYC, due diligence, recognising red flags, and escalating concerns via ISTR
D) Approving business relationships with PEPs

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** As the first line of defence, you are responsible for client-facing AML activities: collecting KYC documentation, performing appropriate due diligence, recognising red flags and suspicious indicators, and escalating concerns to the Compliance Officer via the Internal Suspicious Transaction Report (ISTR). Filing external reports (STR/SAR) is the Compliance Officer's role (second line). Independent auditing is the third line. PEP approvals are senior management's responsibility.

### Question 2
**A potential client is a Myanmar national with a valid UAE residence visa who wants to purchase an apartment in Dubai Marina. What should you do?**

A) Proceed with standard CDD since they have a valid UAE visa
B) Apply Enhanced Due Diligence due to their nationality
C) Decline the relationship immediately — Myanmar nationals are on Prime Capital's prohibited list
D) Escalate to the Compliance Officer for a risk assessment decision

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Myanmar nationals are on Prime Capital's prohibited client list — a direct result of Myanmar being on the FATF blacklist. This is an absolute prohibition with no exceptions. No level of senior management approval can override it. You must decline the relationship immediately, notify the Compliance Officer, and document the interaction.

### Question 3
**You file an Internal Suspicious Transaction Report (ISTR) about a client's unusual payment behaviour. After investigation, the Compliance Officer determines it was a false alarm — the activity was legitimate. Can you face disciplinary action for filing the report?**

A) Yes — false reports waste company resources and damage client relationships
B) No — the no-retaliation policy protects employees who report in good faith
C) Only if this is the third false alarm you have filed
D) It depends on whether the client complained

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Prime Capital's no-retaliation policy (Policy s.21) explicitly protects employees who report suspicious activity in good faith. Even if the report proves unfounded, you will not face disciplinary action, negative consequences, or adverse treatment. This protection exists specifically to encourage reporting — it is always better to file an ISTR that proves unnecessary than to ignore a genuine indicator.

### Question 4
**How frequently does the Compliance Officer report to the Board on AML/CFT compliance status?**

A) Monthly
B) Quarterly
C) Bi-annually (twice per year)
D) Annually

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Per Prime Capital's Policy (s.6.5), the Compliance Officer provides bi-annual compliance reports to the Board. These reports cover the firm's overall compliance status, any incidents or issues identified, training completion, and regulatory developments. This bi-annual reporting ensures senior management maintains visibility into AML/CFT compliance.

### Question 5
**A wealthy client is referred to you by a senior partner. During onboarding, you discover the client operates an unlicensed hawala business. The senior partner says "just proceed, this is a VIP client." What should you do?**

A) Proceed — senior management has effectively approved the relationship
B) Apply Enhanced Due Diligence and document the senior partner's approval
C) Decline the relationship and notify the Compliance Officer — unlicensed hawala operators are prohibited clients
D) Request written approval from the senior partner before proceeding

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Unlicensed hawala operators are on Prime Capital's prohibited client list. Prohibited relationships are absolute — no exceptions, no senior override, and no amount of business justification changes this. You must decline the relationship immediately, notify the Compliance Officer, and document the interaction. A senior partner's instruction does not override a compliance prohibition.
