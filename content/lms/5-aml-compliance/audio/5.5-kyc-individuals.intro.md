Welcome to Module 5.5. This is where compliance becomes practical. KYC — Know Your Customer — is the process by which you establish a client's identity, verify their documents, and assess the risk they present. For individual clients, this means completing a detailed form, collecting seven specific documents, and asking questions that many consultants find uncomfortable.

Here is the reality: the source of funds question is where most consultants struggle. A client spending two million dirhams on a property will naturally wonder why you need to know where the money comes from. Your job is to ask professionally, frame it as standard regulatory procedure, and collect the information. Module 5.5 shows you exactly how.

You will learn the full KYC form — every required field, from personal information through financial details to PEP declarations. You will learn the difference between source of funds and source of wealth — they are not the same question, and both are required. And you will learn how to handle the client who pushes back.

KYC is not paperwork. It is your first opportunity to assess whether a client is legitimate.
