Welcome to Module 5.2. Now that you understand what money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing are, you need to know the legal framework that governs your obligations.

The primary law is Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 — this is the Decree Law that defines everything from your CDD obligations to the penalties for violations. But it does not operate alone. Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 provides the implementing regulations, and critically, its Article 3 is what classifies you — a real estate agent — as a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession. That classification is the reason this entire competency exists.

In this module, you will map the full regulatory landscape: the laws, the cabinet decisions, the circulars, and the regulatory authorities. You will learn that the Ministry of Economy supervises your AML compliance, that the EOCN manages sanctions alerts, and that the FIU receives your reports through goAML.

You will also learn the 8 core AML/CFT obligations that apply to you as a DNFBP — from day one, with no exemptions. And you will understand how the FATF grey list and black list directly affect how you handle international clients.

Know the framework. It is the foundation of everything that follows.
