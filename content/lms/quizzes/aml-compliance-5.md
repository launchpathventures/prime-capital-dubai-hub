# AML Compliance Quiz: KYC for Individual Clients

**Purpose:** Verify your ability to perform KYC procedures for individual clients, collect required documents, and handle sensitive compliance questions professionally.

**Format:** 5 multiple-choice questions
**Passing Score:** 80% (4 out of 5 correct)
**Time Limit:** No time limit (typically 8 minutes)
**Retakes:** Unlimited

---

## Questions

### Question 1
**A client asks "Why do you need to know where my money comes from? No other agent asked me this." What is the best response?**

A) "You're right, I'll skip that question — we can come back to it later"
B) "This is required by UAE federal law for all property transactions. Every client goes through this process. It's handled confidentially."
C) "I need it for my records, but you can give me a rough answer"
D) "I'll note that you declined to answer and we can proceed"

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Frame source of funds inquiries as standard regulatory procedure that applies to all clients. This normalises the question, removes personal implication, and ensures compliance. Skipping the question or accepting vague answers is a compliance failure. Proceeding without the answer violates KYC requirements.

---

### Question 2
**What is the key difference between "source of funds" and "source of wealth"?**

A) Source of funds refers to salary; source of wealth refers to savings
B) Source of funds is where the money for this specific transaction comes from; source of wealth is how the client accumulated their overall wealth
C) Source of funds applies to cash transactions; source of wealth applies to bank transfers
D) They are the same question asked in different ways for verification

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Source of funds answers "Where is the money for THIS transaction coming from?" (e.g., property sale proceeds, salary savings). Source of wealth answers "How did the client build their overall financial position?" (e.g., career as a surgeon, founded a company). Both are required on the KYC form, and source of wealth documentation is required for PEPs and high-risk clients as part of EDD.

### Question 3
**How many documents are required for individual client onboarding at Prime Capital?**

A) 3 — KYC form, passport copy, and source of funds document
B) 5 — KYC form, undertaking, passport, source of funds, and source of wealth
C) 7 — KYC form, source of fund undertaking, passport/visa/EID, POA holder ID (if applicable), source of funds document, source of wealth documentation, and third-party undertaking
D) 10 — all compliance documents plus property-specific paperwork

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Seven documents are required: (1) KYC form, (2) Source of fund undertaking (signed), (3) Valid passport/visa/EID, (4) POA holder ID if applicable, (5) Source of funds document (bank statement/income proof — required for cash, PEP, high-risk), (6) Source of wealth documentation (PEP/high-risk), and (7) Third-party undertaking with supporting docs if applicable.

### Question 4
**During KYC, a client declares they are not a PEP. However, Cygnus Scan screening identifies them as the spouse of a government minister. What should you do?**

A) Accept the client's declaration — they said they are not a PEP
B) Confront the client about their false declaration and refuse service
C) Inform the Compliance Officer about the discrepancy and await guidance — do not confront the client aggressively
D) Override the screening result since the client denied PEP status

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** When screening results conflict with a client's self-declaration, inform the Compliance Officer. The CO will determine next steps, which may include applying EDD, requesting additional information, or declining the relationship. Do not accept the declaration at face value (screening has identified PEP status) and do not confront the client aggressively (this could constitute tipping off if an investigation follows).

---

### Question 5
**A non-resident client wants to purchase property entirely through a Power of Attorney holder. They will not attend any meetings or viewings in person. Which statement is correct?**

A) This is standard practice — proceed with normal CDD on the POA holder only
B) Non-face-to-face transactions are higher risk — apply EDD, verify both the client and POA holder, and collect additional documentation
C) Refuse the transaction — all clients must appear in person
D) Proceed if the POA holder provides their own KYC documentation

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Non-face-to-face transactions are an EDD trigger. The same KYC requirements apply to the actual client (not just the representative), and additional verification steps may be needed. You must collect identification for both the client and the POA holder, verify the POA document's authenticity and scope, and apply enhanced measures. Refusal is not necessary, but heightened scrutiny is required.
