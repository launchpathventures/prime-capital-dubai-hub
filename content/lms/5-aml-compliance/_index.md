# Competency 5: AML Compliance

## Overview

Anti-Money Laundering compliance is not optional — it is a legal obligation under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018. As a real estate professional at Prime Capital, you are classified as a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession (DNFBP), which means you have specific legal duties to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing through property transactions.

This competency covers everything you need to know — from the legal framework and Prime Capital's internal policies, through practical KYC and due diligence procedures, to recognising red flags and filing suspicious activity reports. This is mandatory compliance training required by law and by Prime Capital's AML/CFT Policy V.02.

## What You'll Learn

This competency covers twelve modules in four blocks:

### Block 1: Foundations (Modules 5.1–5.4)
1. **What is ML, TF & PF** — Definitions, stages of money laundering, why real estate is a target
2. **UAE Legal & Regulatory Framework** — The laws, regulations, and authorities that govern your AML obligations
3. **Prime Capital AML Governance & Your Role** — Three lines of defence, your responsibilities as the first line
4. **Risk Assessment & Customer Risk Rating** — How to classify clients using Prime Capital's risk rating tables

### Block 2: KYC & Due Diligence (Modules 5.5–5.8)
5. **KYC for Individual Clients** — Completing individual KYC forms, collecting required documents
6. **KYC for Corporate & Legal Entities** — Corporate KYC, UBO identification, developer onboarding
7. **Customer Due Diligence — SDD, CDD & EDD** — When to apply each level, CDD measures, EDD triggers
8. **PEPs & UBO Identification** — Identifying politically exposed persons, determining ultimate beneficial owners

### Block 3: Sanctions, Red Flags & Reporting (Modules 5.9–5.12)
9. **Sanctions, Screening & Targeted Financial Sanctions** — Cygnus Scan, screening results, TFS implementation
10. **Red Flags & Real Estate ML/FT Typologies** — 60+ red flag indicators, 10 case study typologies
11. **Reporting — goAML, STR/SAR, REAR, ISTR** — Internal and external reporting procedures
12. **Tipping Off, Cash Policy, Record Keeping & Penalties** — What never to say, how long to keep records, penalty framework

## Why This Matters

**For you personally:**
- Violations carry fines from AED 10,000 to AED 50,000,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years
- Tipping off alone carries minimum 6 months imprisonment
- Your career as a licensed broker ends with an AML violation

**For Prime Capital:**
- Companies can be fined AED 500,000 to AED 50,000,000
- A TF conviction means dissolution and closure of the company
- Reputational damage from AML failures is irreversible

**For your clients:**
- Proper compliance protects legitimate clients from being entangled in criminal activity
- Professional AML procedures build trust with sophisticated investors
- Clients respect consultants who take compliance seriously

## Time Commitment

**Estimated Duration:** 6 hours total (360 minutes)

Complete the foundations block (Modules 5.1–5.4) in your first session. Then work through the remaining blocks at a pace that allows you to absorb the material. This is compliance training — understanding matters more than speed.
