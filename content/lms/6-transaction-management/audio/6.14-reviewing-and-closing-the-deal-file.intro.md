When an agent has finished their part, the deal file comes to you. Your job is to check it, say clearly what is wrong, and close it.

Open the deal and go to the Paperwork tab. The top line tells you how many items are missing and how many are waiting for your review. Missing items show first.

For each item, view it or download it. Then accept it, or ask for a new copy. When you ask for a new copy, write a reason the agent can act on. "Passport photo page is cut off, please upload the full page" is useful. "Wrong" is not. If an item doesn't apply to this deal, you can waive it, again with a reason.

Some files have a screening step that only managers see. Record the result: Clear, Escalate or Reject. Escalating or rejecting stops the file from being closed. Your compliance procedures decide what happens next. The app records it; it doesn't make that decision for you.

Download everything gives you every document for the deal in one zip.

Approve becomes available once the agent has finished and every required item is accepted. Until then the page tells you what is outstanding and who has to add it. Or send the whole file back, with a reason.

After approval, move the file to Aftercare with a short reason. It stays on the record, and from then on the agent can read the deal but not change it.

One more thing: billing is for managers only. Those steps are recorded checkpoints. They don't create an invoice, and they don't stop a deal from closing.

The key takeaway: give clear reasons, keep the screening honest, and close the file with a record of why.
