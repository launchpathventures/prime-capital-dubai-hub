# Reviewing and Closing the Deal File in TopStreet AI (Admins)

## Introduction

When an agent has added what their part needs, the file goes to the admin team. This lesson is how you check it, say clearly what is wrong, and close it. Everything is on the deal page, with what is missing shown first.

**Time:** about 25 minutes, with the app open beside you.

---

## 1. Find the deal

1. Open **Deals**. Managers and admins see every agent's deals.
2. Open the deal and use its **Paperwork** tab.

The top line says how many items are missing and how many are waiting for you to review.

<!-- SCREENSHOT 1: Paperwork tab with the top line, Prime branding -->

---

## 2. Review each item

Each row shows what it is, whether it is there and who added it.

- **View** opens it. **Download** saves it.
- **Accept** it when it is right.
- **Ask for a new copy** when it is not, and write a short reason.
- **Waive** an item that does not apply to this deal, with a reason.

The agent is told, and sees your reason on the deal.

**Write reasons the agent can act on.**

| Weak | Better |
|------|--------|
| "Wrong" | "Passport photo page is cut off at the bottom. Please upload the full page." |
| "Redo" | "Emirates ID expires next month. Please upload the new one." |

<!-- SCREENSHOT 2: item row with Accept / Ask for a new copy -->

---

## 3. Record the screening

Some files have a screening step that only managers see. Press **Record the screening** and choose:

- **Clear**
- **Escalate**
- **Reject**

Add a note if you want one. **Escalating or rejecting stops the file from being closed.** Your agency's AML and compliance procedures (see Competency 5) decide what you do next. TopStreet AI records the result; it does not make the compliance decision for you.

---

## 4. Download the paperwork

**Download everything** at the top of the file gives you every document for the deal in one zip.

---

## 5. Approve, or send back

- **Approve** is available once every required item is accepted. Until then the page says what is still outstanding and who has to add it.
- **Send back** returns the whole file to the agent, with a reason, once they have sent it.

---

## 6. Move it to Aftercare

After approval the page says the file is complete. Press **Move to Aftercare** and give a short reason. The reason stays on the record. After that an agent can read the deal but not change it.

<!-- SCREENSHOT 3: Approve and Move to Aftercare -->

---

## Good to know

- **Billing is for managers only.** The billing steps (payment request, billed, method, tax invoice) are checkpoints recorded with who changed them and when. They do not create an invoice and they do not stop a deal from closing.
- **Reviewers are told when a file is complete**: in the app, by email, and by phone if they have allowed notifications. Turn on phone notifications (Settings, then allow notifications).
- **A replacement copy never deletes the old one.** The history keeps both.
- **Older deals** keep working as they did. A deal gets the new list when its agent saves the deal type.

---

## Common mistakes

1. Asking for a new copy without a reason, so the agent has to ask what is wrong
2. Waiving an item because it is awkward, not because it does not apply
3. Recording a screening result without a note when it is Escalate or Reject
4. Moving a file to Aftercare without a clear reason
5. Leaving phone notifications off as a reviewer

---

## Key takeaways

1. Missing items show first: start there
2. Every "ask for a new copy" and every waive needs a reason the agent can act on
3. Escalate or Reject on the screening blocks closing
4. **Download everything** gives you the whole file in one zip
5. **Approve** waits until the agent has finished and every required item is accepted
6. **Move to Aftercare** needs a reason, and it stays on the record
