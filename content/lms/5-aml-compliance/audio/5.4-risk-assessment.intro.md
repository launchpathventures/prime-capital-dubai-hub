Welcome to Module 5.4. This module is the bridge between understanding AML and doing AML. Risk assessment determines everything that follows — the level of due diligence you apply, how frequently you review the client, and whether the relationship should exist at all.

Not all clients carry the same risk. A UAE-resident salaried employee purchasing a home is fundamentally different from a non-resident self-employed individual from a FATF grey-list country purchasing through a corporate vehicle. The risk-based approach means you apply proportionate measures — more scrutiny where risk is higher, streamlined processes where risk is lower.

In this module, you will learn Prime Capital's specific risk rating tables — the exact classifications for individual and entity clients. You need to know these. A salaried Iranian national is high-risk. A free zone company is high-risk. A government entity is low-risk. Getting the classification wrong means every subsequent step is wrong.

You will also learn the prohibited client types — North Korea and Myanmar nationals, arms dealers, unlicensed hawala operators, and others who must never be onboarded under any circumstances. And you will learn the CDD review frequencies: low risk every two years, medium risk every year, high risk every six months.

Risk assessment is not a box-ticking exercise. It is the decision that drives your entire AML workflow for each client.
