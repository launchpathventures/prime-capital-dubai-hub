# Sanctions, Screening & Targeted Financial Sanctions

## Introduction

Sanctions screening is the process of checking whether any party to a transaction appears on international sanctions lists. A match means the person or entity is designated — they are subject to asset freezing, transaction prohibitions, and mandatory reporting. This module covers the screening process, how to interpret results, and the Targeted Financial Sanctions (TFS) implementation procedures.

**Critical point:** A confirmed sanctions match triggers a 24-hour deadline to freeze assets and a 5-business-day deadline to report. These are not guidelines — they are legal obligations with criminal penalties for non-compliance.

## Why This Matters

Sanctions violations are among the most serious AML/CFT offences. They carry significant fines and imprisonment, and they attract attention from international enforcement agencies (not just UAE authorities). A single sanctions violation can result in the firm losing its banking relationships, regulatory action, and criminal prosecution of individuals involved.

---

## What Are Sanctions?

Sanctions are restrictions imposed by governments and international bodies on persons, entities, and countries that are involved in or support terrorism, weapons proliferation, human rights abuses, or other threats to international peace and security.

**Sanctions lists** are databases of designated persons and entities. The key lists for Prime Capital:

| List | Issuing Authority | Scope |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| **UN Consolidated List** | United Nations Security Council | Global — all UN member states must implement |
| **UAE Local Terrorist List** | UAE authorities | UAE-specific designations |
| **OFAC SDN List** | US Treasury | US-administered, but has global reach due to dollar system |
| **EU Consolidated List** | European Union | EU member states |
| **HM Treasury** | United Kingdom | UK-administered |
| **Canada** | Canadian government | Canadian sanctions |

## Prime Capital Screening Operations (Policy s.11.2)

### Who Must Be Screened

Prime Capital screens:

- All **customer databases** — existing clients
- All **transaction parties** — buyers, sellers, intermediaries
- All **potential customers** — before any relationship is established
- All **beneficial owners** — persons behind corporate structures
- All persons with **direct or indirect relationships** to the transaction
- All **directors, agents, and POA holders**

### When Screening Occurs

- **Before any transaction** or business relationship
- **Continuously** — existing customers are regularly re-screened
- **When sanctions lists are updated** — within 24 hours of receiving an update
- **At KYC review intervals** — aligned with CDD review frequencies
- **Daily database screening** — ongoing automated checks

## Cygnus Scan

**Cygnus Scan** is Prime Capital's screening tool. It provides:

- Real-time screening of customer names against all applicable sanctions lists
- PEP database screening
- Adverse media monitoring
- Automated risk scoring based on screening results

### Key Screening Identifiers

When screening, the following identifiers are used:

**For natural persons:**
- Full name (including all known aliases)
- Date of birth
- Nationality
- ID/passport number
- Last known address

**For legal persons:**
- Entity/company name
- Trade license number
- Address of registration
- Address of branches

### Screening Approach

1. **Ownership/control rule** — Entities more than 50% owned or controlled by a listed person are treated as sanctioned themselves
2. **Fuzzy matching** — The system detects close variations in spelling, phonetics, formatting, and transliteration
3. **Weak aliases** — Inclusion assessed based on the overall risk profile rather than name alone

## Understanding Screening Results

| Result | Definition | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Confirmed match** | All key identifiers match a designated person | **Freeze within 24 hours**, file CNMR within 5 business days |
| **Partial/potential match** | Some identifiers match but cannot confirm or rule out | **Suspend immediately**, file PNMR within 5 business days |
| **False positive** | On examination, clearly not the same person | Document internally, no external report |
| **Negative match** | No match found | Proceed with normal CDD |

### Confirmed Match — Detailed Procedure

When screening confirms a match to a designated person on the UAE Local Terrorist List or UN Consolidated List:

1. **Freeze all funds and assets within 24 hours** — No prior notice to the client
2. **Prohibit** making any funds, services, or economic resources available
3. File **CNMR (Confirmed Name Match Report)** via goAML **within 5 business days**
4. **Permitted additions** (interest on accounts, payments under pre-existing contracts) must be **immediately frozen upon receipt**
5. Continue freeze **indefinitely** until the person is delisted or EOCN communicates cancellation

**For an existing customer:** Freeze assets, prohibit services, file CNMR.
**For a potential customer:** Reject the transaction, file CNMR.

### Partial Match — Detailed Procedure

When screening returns a partial match — some identifiers match but you cannot confirm or rule out:

**For an existing customer:**
1. **Suspend transactions immediately**
2. Withhold funds, assets, and services
3. File **PNMR (Partial Name Match Report)** via goAML **within 5 business days**
4. Maintain suspension **until EOCN responds**:
   - If EOCN confirms false positive → lift suspension
   - If EOCN confirms match → freeze assets and file CNMR

**For a potential customer:**
1. Attempt to obtain additional identification **within 10 business days**
2. If identification obtained → re-screen and act accordingly
3. If identification not obtained within 10 days → reject the transaction, file PNMR within 5 business days

## Targeted Financial Sanctions (TFS)

### What Are TFS?

Targeted Financial Sanctions are specific sanctions measures directed at designated persons, entities, and groups:

- **Asset freezing** — All funds and economic resources owned or controlled by the designated person are frozen
- **Prohibition** — It is prohibited to make any funds, services, or economic resources available to designated persons

### TFS Scope

TFS applies to:
- Listed individuals, entities, and groups
- Entities **owned or controlled** by listed persons (50%+ ownership)
- Entities **acting on behalf of** listed persons

### The TFS 4-Step Implementation

**Step 1: Register**

Prime Capital is registered with the **EOCN Notification Alert System (NAS)**. This system sends automated alerts when sanctions lists are updated — ensuring the firm always has the latest designated person information.

**Step 2: Screen**

Screening must occur:
- **Within 24 hours** of receiving a list update
- **Before onboarding** any new client
- At every **KYC review**
- **Before any transaction**
- Through **daily database screening**

**Step 3: Implement TFS Measures**

Upon confirmed match:
- **Freeze** all funds and assets within 24 hours — no prior notice
- **Prohibit** making any funds, services, or economic resources available
- Permitted additions (interest, contractual payments) are **immediately frozen upon receipt**

**Step 4: Report**

File the appropriate report via goAML within 5 business days:
- Confirmed match → CNMR
- Partial match → PNMR

---

## Screening During Weekends and Holidays

If sanctions list updates are received during weekends or public holidays:

- If no transactions occur during this period, screening must commence **immediately on the next business day**
- If transactions are in progress, procedures must be in place to screen before completion
- Automated systems should process updates regardless of business hours

## Other Sanctions Regimes (Policy s.13)

For matches against lists other than UN Consolidated List and UAE Local Terrorist List (e.g., OFAC, EU, HM Treasury, INTERPOL):

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Suspicious activity NOT involving UN/UAE list match | File **STR/SAR** with FIU |
| Confirmed/partial match to UN/UAE list | File **CNMR or PNMR** |
| Match to OFAC, EU, HMT, or INTERPOL | **Consult Compliance Officer** — CO determines appropriate action and engagement with supervisory authority |

---

## Lifting of Freezing Measures

Freezing measures can only be lifted when:

1. The designated person is **delisted** from the applicable sanctions list, OR
2. **EOCN communicates cancellation** of the freezing directive through goAML

**You cannot unilaterally lift a freeze.** Even if the client provides evidence they believe exonerates them, the freeze remains until official delisting or EOCN cancellation.

## Prohibited Business Relationships — Sanctions Context

Prime Capital is prohibited from maintaining business relationships with any targets from:

- UAE Local Terrorist List
- UN Consolidated List
- EU Consolidated List
- UK HM Treasury
- OFAC SDN List
- EOCN NAS designations
- Security Council Committee designations
