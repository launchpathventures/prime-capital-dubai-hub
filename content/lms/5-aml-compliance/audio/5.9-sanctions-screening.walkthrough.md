Let me walk you through three different screening results and the exact workflow for each. This is the decision tree you follow every time Cygnus Scan returns a result.

---

## Result 1: Negative Match — No Hit

**Scenario:** You screen a new client, Ms. Aisha Rahman, Bangladeshi national, UAE resident, salaried employee at a bank. Cygnus Scan returns no matches against any sanctions list, PEP database, or adverse media source.

**Step 1:** Confirm the screening was run against all applicable lists — UN, UAE Local Terrorist List, OFAC, EU, HMT.

**Step 2:** Record the screening result in the client file. Note the date, the lists screened, and the result: negative.

**Step 3:** Proceed with normal CDD based on the client's risk classification. Ms. Rahman is a salaried UAE resident from a country not on the FATF grey or black list — likely low or medium risk.

**Step 4:** Schedule the next screening for the CDD review date. If she is low risk, that is in 2 years. If medium, 1 year.

**Key point:** A negative result does not mean the client is permanently clear. They will be re-screened at every CDD review, whenever sanctions lists are updated, and if any new information emerges.

---

## Result 2: Partial Match — Some Identifiers Match

**Scenario:** You screen a potential client, Mr. Omar Farooq, Pakistani national, non-resident. Cygnus Scan returns a partial match: the name "Omar Farooq" appears on the UN Consolidated List. Nationality matches (Pakistani). But the date of birth and passport number do not match. You cannot confirm or rule out that this is the same person.

**Step 1: Suspend immediately.** If there are any pending transactions or commitments for this client, suspend them now. Do not wait for further analysis.

**Step 2: Compile comparison data.** Document side by side:
- Client: Omar Farooq, DOB 15 March 1982, Pakistani, Passport AB1234567
- Listed person: Omar Farooq, DOB 22 July 1970, Pakistani, no passport recorded, aliases include "Omar the Builder"

Note matching identifiers: name, nationality. Note non-matching: DOB, no passport overlap, no alias match.

**Step 3: Escalate to Compliance Officer.** Provide the comparison data and state: "Partial match — cannot confirm or rule out. Transactions suspended."

**Step 4: The CO files a PNMR** via goAML within 5 business days of the screening result.

**Step 5: Wait for EOCN response.** Do not proceed with any business activity until the EOCN responds to the PNMR.

**If EOCN confirms false positive:** Lift the suspension. Resume normal onboarding. Document the EOCN response in the file.

**If EOCN confirms match:** The partial match becomes a confirmed match. Move to the confirmed match workflow — freeze all assets within 24 hours, file CNMR.

**For a potential customer (not yet onboarded):** You may attempt to obtain additional identification within 10 business days to resolve the match. If you obtain enough identifiers to conclusively determine it is not the same person, the CO may determine it is a false positive. If you cannot obtain sufficient identification within 10 days, reject the transaction and file the PNMR.

---

## Result 3: Confirmed Match — All Identifiers Match

**Scenario:** During a daily database screening, Cygnus Scan flags an existing client, Mr. Ali Hassan Mohammed, as a confirmed match on the UAE Local Terrorist List. The name, date of birth, nationality, and passport number all match the designated person. The listing was added to the UAE Local Terrorist List yesterday.

**Step 1: Freeze all funds and assets within 24 hours.** No prior notice to the client. This includes:
- Any deposits held by or on behalf of the client
- Any pending transaction proceeds
- Any property in the client's name (notify relevant authorities)
- Any services you are providing on the client's behalf

**Step 2: Prohibit all activity.** Make no funds, services, or economic resources available to the client. This means:
- Cancel all scheduled viewings, meetings, and activities
- Do not process any pending transactions
- Do not release any documents, keys, or property access
- Do not communicate with the client about the freeze (tipping off risk)

**Step 3: Handle permitted additions.** If any payments are due to the client under pre-existing contracts (interest, rental income, etc.), those payments must be immediately frozen upon receipt. They are not released to the client.

**Step 4: The CO files a CNMR** via goAML within 5 business days. The CNMR must include:
- Full name and identification documents of the matched person
- Amount of frozen funds and assets, with proof
- Description of freezing actions taken

**Step 5: Maintain the freeze indefinitely.** The freeze remains in place until:
- The person is delisted from the sanctions list, OR
- EOCN communicates cancellation through goAML

Nobody at Prime Capital can lift the freeze. Not the CO, not senior management, not the client's lawyer.

**Step 6: If the client contacts you,** keep communication neutral. You can acknowledge receiving their call but you cannot discuss the freeze, the reason for it, or any investigation. Refer all inquiries to the Compliance Officer. Be aware that anything you say could constitute tipping off.

---

## Summary Decision Tree

```
Screening Result
├── Negative → Document, proceed with CDD, schedule re-screening
├── False Positive → Document analysis internally, no external report
├── Partial Match → Suspend → Escalate to CO → PNMR within 5 days → Wait for EOCN
└── Confirmed Match → Freeze within 24 hours → CNMR within 5 days → Maintain indefinitely
```

The deadlines are absolute. The freeze is mandatory. The documentation is non-negotiable. Follow the tree.
