Welcome to Module 5.1. This is where we start — with the legal definitions of the three financial crimes you are obligated to prevent: money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing.

Here is why this matters to you personally. Under UAE law, money laundering is not a vague concept. It is defined by six specific acts in Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025. If you transfer, move, conceal, acquire, possess, or use proceeds that you know are derived from a crime, you have committed money laundering. And here is the critical part: ML is prosecuted independently of the predicate offence. Even if the original crime goes unpunished, the laundering itself is a separate crime.

In this module, you will learn how dirty money moves through real estate in three stages — placement, layering, and integration. You will understand why terrorist financing is harder to detect than ML — because the funds can come from legitimate sources. And you will learn why Dubai's property market is one of the most attractive sectors globally for money laundering.

This is foundational knowledge. Every module that follows builds on what you learn here.
