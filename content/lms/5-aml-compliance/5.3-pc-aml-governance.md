# Prime Capital AML Governance & Your Role

## Introduction

Understanding the law is essential, but understanding how Prime Capital implements the law is what makes you effective. This module covers our internal AML/CFT governance structure, the three lines of defence model, your specific responsibilities within that model, and the business relationships that are absolutely prohibited.

**This is where compliance becomes personal.** The governance model defines exactly what you are responsible for — not as an abstract concept, but as daily practices that apply to every client interaction.

## Why This Matters

Prime Capital operates a **zero-tolerance policy** toward money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing. This is not a marketing statement. It means that failure to follow AML/CFT procedures — whether through negligence or intent — will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination, in addition to any legal consequences.

---

## Prime Capital's AML/CFT Policy

### Policy Administration

Prime Capital's AML/CFT Policy V.02 (February 2026) is the authoritative internal document governing all AML/CFT matters:

- **Owned by:** The Compliance Officer
- **Approved by:** The Owner (senior management)
- **Review cycle:** Annually at minimum, or when triggered by regulatory changes, significant events, or audit findings
- **Scope:** Applies to all employees, contractors, and representatives of Prime Capital

### Zero-Tolerance Statement

Prime Capital maintains a zero-tolerance approach to money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing. The firm is committed to:

- Full compliance with UAE AML/CFT laws and regulations
- Implementation of robust internal controls and procedures
- Regular training and awareness for all staff
- Prompt reporting of suspicious activity
- Cooperation with regulatory authorities

## The Three Lines of Defence

Prime Capital's AML governance follows the internationally recognised **Three Lines of Defence** model. Understanding this model — and where you sit within it — is critical.

### First Line: Business (You)

**This is you — the front office, the sales team, the consultants who interact with clients.**

As the first line of defence, you are responsible for:

- **Client onboarding KYC** — Collecting identification documents, completing KYC forms, verifying client identity
- **Customer due diligence** — Applying the appropriate level of CDD (simplified, standard, or enhanced) based on risk
- **Risk identification** — Recognising red flags and unusual behaviour during client interactions
- **Initial escalation** — Reporting concerns to the Compliance Officer via the Internal Suspicious Transaction Report (ISTR)
- **Ongoing monitoring** — Noting changes in client behaviour or circumstances that may indicate increased risk
- **Record keeping** — Maintaining accurate, complete client and transaction records

**The critical point:** Money laundering and terrorist financing are most likely to be detected at the point of first contact — which is your desk, your phone call, your meeting. If you miss the indicators, the second and third lines may never see them.

### Second Line: Compliance

**The Compliance Officer / Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO).**

The second line is responsible for:

- Developing and maintaining AML/CFT policies and procedures
- Providing guidance and advice to the first line (you)
- Reviewing ISTRs from employees and determining whether external reports are required
- Filing STR/SAR, CNMR, PNMR, and REAR reports with the FIU via goAML
- Liaising with regulatory authorities (Ministry of Economy, EOCN, FIU)
- Monitoring overall compliance across the firm
- Conducting AML/CFT risk assessments
- Organising training programmes
- Maintaining the firm's compliance programme documentation

**The Compliance Officer's key responsibilities (from Policy s.6.2):**

1. Ensure compliance with applicable AML/CFT legislation
2. Regularly review and update AML/CFT policies and procedures
3. Develop and implement a risk-based AML/CFT programme
4. Act as the primary point of contact for regulatory inquiries
5. Receive and evaluate ISTRs from employees
6. File STRs, SARs, CNMRs, PNMRs, and REARs with the FIU
7. Ensure proper CDD is conducted on all clients
8. Monitor transactions for suspicious activity
9. Coordinate with external parties (regulators, auditors, law enforcement)
10. Maintain AML/CFT records and documentation
11. Provide AML/CFT training and awareness sessions
12. Conduct periodic compliance testing and audits
13. Advise senior management on AML/CFT matters
14. Ensure timely response to sanctions list updates
15. Report to the Board bi-annually on compliance status (Policy s.6.5)

### Third Line: Audit

**Independent audit function — tests the effectiveness of the first and second lines.**

The third line:

- Conducts independent reviews of AML/CFT controls
- Tests whether policies are being followed in practice
- Identifies gaps between policy and implementation
- Reports findings directly to senior management / the Board
- Recommends improvements based on audit findings

**You will not interact with the third line regularly**, but you should know that your compliance practices will be audited. An auditor may review your client files, check your KYC documentation, and verify that you followed the correct CDD procedures.

## Senior Management Responsibilities

Senior management at Prime Capital is responsible for:

- Approving the AML/CFT policy and any amendments
- Allocating adequate resources for compliance
- Ensuring the Compliance Officer has the authority and independence to perform their role
- Reviewing bi-annual compliance reports
- Approving business relationships with PEPs (this cannot be delegated)
- Setting the tone from the top — demonstrating that compliance is a priority

## Prohibited Business Relationships

### Statutory Prohibitions (Policy s.7)

Prime Capital is **prohibited by law** from maintaining business relationships with:

1. **Shell banks** — Banks with no physical presence in any country and no affiliation with a regulated financial group
2. **Sanctioned persons/entities** — Any person or entity on UAE Local Terrorist List, UN Consolidated List, or other applicable sanctions lists
3. **Anonymous or fictitious accounts** — Relationships where the client's true identity cannot be established
4. **Incomplete CDD** — Relationships where satisfactory CDD cannot be completed

### Prime Capital Prohibited Customer Types

In addition to statutory prohibitions, Prime Capital will **not onboard** the following customer types under any circumstances:

- **North Korea (DPRK) nationals**
- **Myanmar nationals**
- **Arms dealers**
- **Unlicensed hawala operators**
- **Persons involved in drug dealing**
- **Unlicensed gambling or casino operators**
- **Unregistered NPOs or trusts**
- **Persons with direct SDN (Specially Designated Nationals) links**

**These are absolute prohibitions.** No level of senior management approval can override them. If a potential client falls into any of these categories, the relationship must be declined immediately and the Compliance Officer must be notified.

## No-Retaliation Policy (Policy s.21)

### Your Protection

Prime Capital has a strict no-retaliation policy: **employees who report suspicious activity in good faith are protected from any form of retaliation.**

This means:

- You will not face disciplinary action for filing an ISTR that turns out to be unfounded, provided it was filed in good faith
- You will not be penalised for declining to proceed with a transaction due to AML concerns
- You will not face negative consequences for escalating concerns to the Compliance Officer
- Management will not take adverse action against employees who raise genuine compliance concerns

### Why This Matters

Without this protection, employees might hesitate to report suspicious activity — especially if the client is high-value or the deal is lucrative. The no-retaliation policy removes that disincentive. **When in doubt, report.** It is always better to file an ISTR that proves unnecessary than to ignore a genuine suspicious indicator.

## Training & Awareness Requirements (Policy s.22)

### Mandatory Training

All Prime Capital employees must:

- Complete AML/CFT training **annually at minimum**
- Pass post-training assessments to verify understanding
- Stay current on regulatory changes and new guidance
- Understand Prime Capital's specific policies and procedures

### This Competency

This competency (Competency 5: AML Compliance) constitutes part of your mandatory AML/CFT training. Completing all 12 modules and passing all quizzes fulfils your initial training requirement. Annual refresher training will follow.

## Know Your Employee (KYE) (Policy s.24)

Prime Capital conducts background checks on all employees as part of its AML/CFT programme:

- Verification of identity and qualifications
- Background screening against sanctions and adverse media
- Assessment of suitability for roles with AML/CFT responsibilities
- Ongoing monitoring of employee conduct

**Why this exists:** An insider threat — an employee who deliberately facilitates ML/TF — is one of the most serious risks to any firm. KYE procedures help prevent this.

---

## Policy Circulation (Policy s.23)

The AML/CFT Policy is circulated to all employees and must be acknowledged:

- New employees receive the policy during onboarding
- All employees are notified of policy updates
- Acknowledgement of receipt and understanding is documented
- The policy is available for reference at all times

## Key Takeaways

1. **You are the first line of defence** — ML/TF detection starts with you at the point of client contact
2. **The Compliance Officer is your escalation point** — report concerns via the ISTR process
3. **Prohibited relationships are absolute** — no exceptions for North Korea/Myanmar nationals, arms dealers, unlicensed hawala, drug dealers, unlicensed gambling, unregistered NPOs, or direct SDN links
4. **The no-retaliation policy protects you** — report in good faith without fear of consequences
5. **Annual AML/CFT training is mandatory** — this competency is part of that requirement
6. **Senior management approves PEP relationships** — this responsibility cannot be delegated
7. **Bi-annual compliance reports** go to the Board — your compliance practices are measured and reported
