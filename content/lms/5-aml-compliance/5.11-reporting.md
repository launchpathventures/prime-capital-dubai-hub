# Reporting — goAML, STR/SAR, REAR, ISTR

## Introduction

When you identify suspicious activity or a sanctions match, reporting it is a legal obligation — not a choice. This module covers the complete reporting chain: from your internal report to the Compliance Officer, through the CO's review, to external reporting via the goAML platform. You will learn the different report types, their triggers, timelines, and what happens after a report is filed.

**Critical point:** There is no minimum monetary threshold for reporting suspicious activity. If you suspect ML/TF, you must report it — regardless of the transaction amount. A AED 10,000 transaction that triggers suspicion requires the same reporting as a AED 10 million one.

## Why This Matters

Failure to report is a criminal offence under Article 24 of Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 — punishable by imprisonment and fines of AED 100,000 to AED 1,000,000. This applies to you personally, not just the firm.

---

## The Reporting Chain

```
You (suspicion) → ISTR → Compliance Officer → Review → External Report (goAML)
```

### Your Role in Reporting

1. You **identify** suspicious activity or red flags
2. You **file an ISTR** (Internal Suspicious Transaction Report) with the Compliance Officer
3. The CO **reviews** the circumstances
4. The CO **determines** whether an external report is required
5. The CO **files** the external report via goAML (if required)
6. The CO **documents** the rationale — whether they report or not

**You do not file external reports.** Your responsibility ends with the ISTR. But you must file it promptly — no later than 24 hours after establishing suspicion.

---

## Internal Reporting — ISTR/ISAR (Policy s.17.2)

### When to File

**ALL employees** must submit an ISTR to the Compliance Officer upon discovering suspicious activity. There is:

- **No minimum threshold** — suspicion alone triggers the obligation
- **No minimum monetary value** — even zero-value transactions can be reported
- **No requirement for proof** — you are reporting suspicion, not evidence
- **A deadline** — submit as soon as suspicion is established, no later than 24 hours

### The ISTR Form (Appendix IV)

Prime Capital's Internal Suspicious Transaction Report form captures:

| Field | What to Provide |
|-------|----------------|
| **Client name** | Full name as per KYC records |
| **Representative** | If acting through a representative, their name |
| **Address** | Client's known address |
| **Phone** | Contact number |
| **Email** | Contact email |
| **Business/Occupation** | Client's declared occupation or business type |
| **Passport/ID/Trade License** | Identification document details |
| **Nationality** | Client's nationality |
| **Business location** | Where the client operates |
| **Nature of suspected transactions** | What type of transaction is involved |
| **Amount of suspected transactions** | Value (if known) |
| **Connected accounts** | Any related accounts or transactions |
| **Reason for suspicion** | Your detailed explanation of why you are suspicious — this is the most important field |
| **CO instructions** | Left blank — the CO completes this after review |

### After Filing the ISTR

Once you submit the ISTR:

1. The CO reviews the circumstances surrounding the reported activity
2. The CO determines whether the suspicion warrants an external report
3. If yes → the CO files the appropriate external report via goAML
4. If no → the CO documents the rationale for not reporting
5. Either way, the ISTR is filed and retained as part of the compliance record

## External Reporting — STR/SAR (Policy s.16, 17.3)

### What Are STR/SAR?

- **STR** = Suspicious Transaction Report (for completed or attempted transactions)
- **SAR** = Suspicious Activity Report (for suspicious behaviour that may not involve a specific transaction)

### When to File

STR/SAR is required for any suspicious transactions or activities that do **NOT** involve confirmed or partial matches to the UAE Local Terrorist List or UN Consolidated List. (Those are reported via CNMR/PNMR instead.)

### Who Files

The **Compliance Officer** files STR/SAR — not you. Your role is to file the ISTR that triggers the CO's review.

### Timeline

- CO must notify the FIU **without delay, within 24 hours** of determining that suspicion is warranted

### After STR Filing

Once an STR is filed:

1. **Follow FIU instructions** — the FIU may request additional information or direct specific actions
2. **Classify the customer as high-risk immediately** — regardless of prior classification
3. **Implement enhanced monitoring** — increased scrutiny of all the client's activities
4. **Delay transaction execution where possible** — without tipping off the client
5. **Retain all STR documents** for a minimum of 5 years from closing of investigation

---

## Confirmed Name Match Report — CNMR (Policy s.17.4)

### When to File

CNMR is required when there is a **confirmed match** to a designated person on the sanctions lists.

### Existing Customer

1. **Freeze all funds and assets within 24 hours** (no prior notice)
2. **Prohibit** making any funds, services, or transfers available
3. File CNMR via goAML **within 5 business days**

### Potential Customer

1. **Reject the transaction**
2. File CNMR via goAML **within 5 business days**

### CNMR Must Include

- Full name and identification documents of the matched person
- Amount of frozen funds/assets with proof
- Description of rejected transaction and/or freezing actions taken

---

## Partial Name Match Report — PNMR (Policy s.17.5)

### When to File

PNMR is required when there is a **partial match** — some identifiers match a designated person, but you cannot confirm or rule out.

### Existing Customer

1. **Suspend transactions immediately**
2. Withhold funds, assets, and services
3. File PNMR via goAML **within 5 business days**
4. Maintain suspension **until EOCN responds:**
   - False positive → lift suspension, resume normal operations
   - Confirmed match → freeze assets, file CNMR

### Potential Customer

1. Attempt to obtain additional identification **within 10 business days**
2. If identification obtained → re-screen and act based on result
3. If identification **not** obtained within 10 days → reject transaction, file PNMR within 5 business days

---

## Real Estate Activity Report — REAR (Policy s.17.1)

### When to File

REAR is required for purchase or sale of freehold real estate involving:

- **Single physical cash transaction ≥ AED 55,000** (or linked transactions totalling this amount)
- **Payment via virtual asset** (any amount)
- **Funds converted from virtual asset**

### Required Information

For the REAR, you must obtain and record:

- Emirates ID or passport copy
- Receipts, invoices, and contracts
- Purchase and sale agreement

If buyer/seller is a legal entity, additionally:
- Trade license
- Articles of association
- Register of beneficial owners
- Emirates ID/passport for all BOs and shareholders

### Where to File

Filed via the **FIU goAML platform** by the Compliance Officer.

## Information Requests from FIU — RFI (Policy s.17.6)

When the FIU requests additional information:

- Prime Capital is **obliged to provide** any requested information
- Information must be **accurate and provided promptly**
- Failure to cooperate with FIU requests is a compliance violation

## Distinguishing Between Report Types

| Report | Trigger | Filed When |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **ISTR** | You suspect ML/TF | Internal — to CO within 24 hours of suspicion |
| **STR/SAR** | CO confirms suspicion, no sanctions match | External — to FIU within 24 hours of CO determination |
| **CNMR** | Confirmed match to sanctions list | External — to FIU within 5 business days |
| **PNMR** | Partial match to sanctions list | External — to FIU within 5 business days |
| **REAR** | Cash ≥ AED 55,000 or virtual asset payment in property transaction | External — to FIU via goAML |

### Key Distinction

- **CNMR/PNMR** = involves confirmed or partial match to a designated person on a sanctions list
- **STR/SAR** = involves identified red flags or suspicion reasons, does **NOT** involve a confirmed or partial sanctions match
- **CNMR/PNMR** relates to an established legal relationship with a designated person or party
- **STR/SAR** does NOT involve an established legal relationship with a designated person

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **ISTR to CO within 24 hours** — this is your primary reporting responsibility
2. **No minimum threshold** — report on suspicion, not on amount
3. **STR/SAR** = suspicious activity without sanctions match; **CNMR/PNMR** = sanctions list match
4. **REAR** = cash ≥ AED 55,000 or virtual asset payments in property transactions
5. **You file internal reports (ISTR)** — the CO files external reports (STR/SAR, CNMR, PNMR, REAR)
6. **After STR filing:** classify as high-risk, enhance monitoring, delay transactions where possible
7. **Failure to report** = criminal offence under Article 24 — imprisonment + fines AED 100K-1M
