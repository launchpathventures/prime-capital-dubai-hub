# Welcome to AML Compliance

Welcome to Competency 5: AML Compliance. I'm your learning coach, and this competency is different from everything you've covered so far. This is not about sales technique or market knowledge. This is about the law.

---

## Why This Competency Exists

As a real estate professional in the UAE, you are classified as a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession — a DNFBP. That classification comes from Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018, and it means you have specific legal obligations to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing through property transactions.

This is not theoretical. Dubai's real estate market is attractive to money launderers precisely because it involves high-value assets, international buyers, and complex transactions. You are the first line of defence. When a client walks into Prime Capital, you are the person who determines whether they are who they say they are, whether their money is legitimate, and whether the transaction makes sense.

Get this wrong, and the consequences are severe. We are talking about fines up to AED 50 million for the company. Imprisonment up to 10 years for individuals. And tipping off — informing a client that they are under suspicion — carries a minimum of 6 months in prison on its own.

---

## What You'll Master

This competency covers twelve modules across three blocks.

In the first block, you will learn the foundations: what money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing actually are, the UAE legal framework that governs your obligations, Prime Capital's own AML governance model and your role within it, and how to assess and rate customer risk.

In the second block, you will develop practical skills: completing KYC for individual and corporate clients, applying the right level of due diligence — simplified, standard, or enhanced — and identifying politically exposed persons and ultimate beneficial owners.

In the third block, you will learn the operational procedures: sanctions screening using Cygnus Scan, recognising red flags from Prime Capital's list of over 60 indicators, filing reports through goAML, and understanding the tipping off prohibitions and penalty framework.

## How to Approach This

Take this competency seriously. Every module has its own quiz, and you need 80% on each to pass. But more importantly, this is knowledge that protects you, your clients, and the firm.

When you are working through the modules, think about the clients you will meet. The vast majority of your clients will be legitimate investors. But AML compliance exists for the ones who are not — and your ability to spot the difference is what makes you a professional.
