## Questions

### Question 1
**A colleague casually mentions to another agent at lunch: "We had to file a report on that Russian investor's transaction last week." Is this a problem?**

A) No — colleagues within the same firm can discuss compliance matters freely
B) Yes — this constitutes tipping off if the information reaches the client, and discussing STR details outside the compliance function violates the confidentiality requirement
C) Only if the other agent tells the client
D) No — it is only tipping off if the client is told directly

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Tipping off applies to informing ANY person — not just the client — that a report has been filed or an investigation is underway. Discussing STR details outside the compliance function violates Prime Capital's confidentiality requirements. Even if the colleague does not tell the client, the disclosure itself is a violation. STR content and the act of reporting must be kept strictly confidential.

### Question 2
**What is the minimum penalty for tipping off under UAE law?**

A) A warning letter from the Compliance Officer
B) Fine of AED 10,000
C) Fine of AED 100,000 to AED 500,000 AND imprisonment of at least 6 months
D) Fine of AED 50,000 and a 3-month suspension

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Under Article 25 of Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018, tipping off carries fines of AED 100,000 to AED 500,000 AND imprisonment of at least 6 months. There is no fine-only option or suspended sentence — imprisonment is mandatory. Additionally, Prime Capital will terminate the employee. This makes tipping off one of the most severely penalised AML violations.

### Question 3
**A client wants to pay a AED 80,000 booking deposit in cash. What is Prime Capital's policy?**

A) Accept the cash — it is below AED 100,000
B) Decline the cash and explain that Prime Capital discourages cash above AED 55,000; redirect to bank transfer or cheque, and note that a REAR may be required
C) Accept the cash but split it into two payments of AED 40,000 each
D) Accept the cash if the client provides a passport copy

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Prime Capital discourages cash payments above AED 55,000. For amounts at or above this threshold, traceable payment methods (bank transfer or cheque) must be used. AED 80,000 exceeds the threshold, so you should redirect the client to a bank transfer. A REAR is also triggered for cash payments ≥ AED 55,000 in property transactions. Splitting the payment (option C) would itself be a red flag (structuring).

### Question 4
**How long must CDD and transaction records be retained at Prime Capital?**

A) 1 year from the date of transaction
B) 3 years from the end of the business relationship
C) 5 years from the end of the business relationship or date of transaction, whichever is later
D) 10 years from the date of onboarding

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** All CDD documents, transaction records, compliance records, and AML programme documentation must be retained for a minimum of 5 years from either the end of the business relationship or the date of the transaction — whichever is later. Records must be kept securely and remain accessible for inspection by regulators, auditors, or law enforcement.

### Question 5
**Under Article 23 of the Federal Decree-Law, what is the maximum fine a company (legal person) can face for money laundering?**

A) AED 5,000,000
B) AED 10,000,000
C) AED 50,000,000
D) AED 100,000,000

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Under Article 23(1), legal persons (companies) convicted of money laundering face fines of AED 500,000 to AED 50,000,000. Additionally, under Article 23(2), a TF conviction can result in dissolution and closure of the company — effectively the corporate death penalty. These penalties underscore why AML compliance is not just an individual obligation but an existential risk for the firm.
