You've won the deal. Now your admin team needs your details and a set of documents to take it to completion. Chasing those by message wastes your time and theirs.

In TopStreet AI, every won deal has one short list at the top called "What this file needs". It tells you exactly what is still missing. Each row has a status word and one button, and the highlighted button is your next step.

First, add the deal details. Deal type, lead type, commission and lead source. When you change anything, a bar appears at the bottom of the page. Press Save changes. Nothing is kept until you do.

Second, add the documents. Press the row's button, choose the file, and the row turns to Done. Check the whole page is visible, the scan is readable, the name matches the buyer, and the document is current.

Third, you don't send anything. When your part is complete, the file goes to your admin team by itself. Items marked Later are only needed when the deal closes.

If your manager asks for a new copy, you'll be told in the app, and the row shows their reason. Add the new copy on the same row. The old one is kept in the history, so nothing is lost. Turn on phone notifications so you hear straight away.

And if an older deal asks "What kind of deal is this?", choose the type that fits and save. If you're not sure, ask your manager rather than guessing.

The key takeaway: one list, one Save, and the file goes on by itself. Keep it complete and accurate, and your admin team has what they need.
