Welcome to Module 5.11. You have learned how to identify suspicious activity. Now you need to know how to report it. The reporting chain at Prime Capital has two stages: your internal report to the Compliance Officer, and the CO's external report to the FIU via the goAML platform.

Your primary tool is the ISTR — the Internal Suspicious Transaction Report. When you suspect ML or TF, you file an ISTR with the CO within 24 hours. That is your obligation. The CO then reviews the circumstances and decides whether to file an external report — an STR/SAR for suspicious activity, a CNMR for a confirmed sanctions match, a PNMR for a partial match, or a REAR for qualifying cash transactions.

There is no minimum monetary threshold for reporting suspicion. A AED 10,000 transaction that triggers genuine concern requires the same reporting as a AED 10 million one. And failure to report is a standalone crime — imprisonment plus fines of AED 100,000 to AED 1 million.

In this module, you will learn the ISTR form fields, the distinction between each external report type, the timelines, and what happens after a report is filed. When in doubt, report. The no-retaliation policy protects you.
