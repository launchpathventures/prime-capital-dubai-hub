## Questions

### Question 1
**A salaried Iranian national with a valid UAE residence visa wants to purchase a property. Using Prime Capital's individual client risk rating table, what is their risk classification?**

A) Low — they are salaried and residing in the UAE
B) Medium — they are residing in the UAE with regular employment
C) High — Iran nationals in the UAE are classified as high-risk regardless of employment status
D) Prohibited — Iran nationals cannot be onboarded

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** According to Prime Capital's risk rating table, Iran and Russian nationals residing in the UAE are classified as high-risk — regardless of their employment status. This is different from the prohibited list (North Korea and Myanmar nationals are prohibited). Iran nationals can be onboarded but require Enhanced Due Diligence, senior management approval, and more frequent CDD reviews (every 6 months).

### Question 2
**A free zone company registered in Dubai wants to purchase commercial property. Using Prime Capital's entity risk rating table, what is the company's risk classification?**

A) Low — it is registered in Dubai
B) Medium — commercial property is standard business
C) High — free zone companies are classified as high-risk entities
D) It depends on the company's revenue

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Free zone companies are explicitly classified as high-risk in Prime Capital's entity risk rating table. This is because free zone structures can involve complex ownership, foreign shareholders, and reduced regulatory oversight compared to mainland companies. A free zone company purchasing property requires Enhanced Due Diligence in addition to standard CDD measures.

### Question 3
**A high-risk client completed CDD onboarding 8 months ago. According to Prime Capital's CDD review frequencies, what action is required?**

A) No action needed — the next review is at the 1-year mark
B) The CDD review is overdue — high-risk clients must be reviewed every 6 months
C) Send a reminder to the client to update their documents within 30 days
D) Schedule a review for the 12-month anniversary of onboarding

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** High-risk clients must have their CDD reviewed every 6 months. At 8 months, this review is 2 months overdue. The CDD review frequencies are: Low risk = every 2 years, Medium risk = every 1 year, High risk = every 6 months. Overdue reviews should be escalated to the Compliance Officer and completed as soon as possible.

### Question 4
**Which of the following is NOT one of the 8 ML risk factors specific to real estate identified in Prime Capital's policy?**

A) Complex ownership structures obscuring the true buyer
B) High-value cash transactions in property purchases
C) Properties located in residential zones rather than commercial
D) Rapid purchase and resale of properties at unusual prices

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Property location (residential vs commercial) is not one of the 8 ML risk factors. The actual factors are: (1) high-value cash, (2) complex ownership structures, (3) third-party payments, (4) rapid purchase and resale, (5) non-face-to-face transactions, (6) high-risk jurisdictions, (7) over- or under-valuation, and (8) unusual source of funds. Both residential and commercial properties can be exploited for ML.

### Question 5
**A self-employed UAE resident runs an import/export business and wants to purchase property through his company. How would you assess the overall risk?**

A) Low — the individual is a UAE resident and the company is UAE-registered
B) Medium — self-employed individuals are medium risk
C) High — both the individual (self-employed with high-risk business line) and the entity (import/export) are high-risk
D) Moderate — average the individual and entity risk levels

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Both factors point to high risk. For the individual: self-employed persons with a high-risk business line are classified as high-risk in Prime Capital's individual table. For the entity: import/export companies are explicitly listed as high-risk in the entity table. When multiple risk factors converge, the overall risk does not decrease — it should reflect the highest applicable level. This client and entity both require EDD.
